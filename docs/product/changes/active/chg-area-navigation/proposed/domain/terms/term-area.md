## Definition

A named destination grouping related information and actions within Pintatónica's public presence or private Backstage workspace. Navigation identifies the currently presented area.

## Distinguish From

A separate band, business bounded context, permission role or data publication decision. A public repertoire/concert area and a private repertoire/concert area can share a label without exposing the same information.

## Usage

Public areas are Inicio, La banda, Repertorio, Media, Conciertos and Contacto, presented as linkable sections of one scrolling page. Private areas are Inicio, Ensayos, Repertorio, Setlists and Conciertos. Private areas have separate pages and are reached through member-only secondary navigation.
