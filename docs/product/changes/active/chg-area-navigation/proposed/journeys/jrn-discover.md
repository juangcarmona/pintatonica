## Intended Outcome

Discover the band, music, media, upcoming gigs and a contact route.

## Entry Conditions

A visitor can reach the public website.

## Journey Narrative

Open the band site, learn who Pintatónica is, explore deliberate repertoire/media selections and find gig or contact information.

Move through the public Inicio, La banda, Repertorio, Media, Conciertos and Contacto sections of one scrolling page while navigation identifies the currently visible area after scrolling or link activation. Share section links and return through browser history. Backstage is visibly a separate member-only login/entry action; public discovery remains anonymous.

## Variants and Branches

Direct links may start at a gig or repertoire item; private content stays outside this journey.

## Completion Conditions

The visitor has found the published information they seek without requiring private membership.
