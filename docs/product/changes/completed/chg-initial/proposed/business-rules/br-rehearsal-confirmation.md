## Rule

Any active member may explicitly confirm a candidate time as a shared rehearsal after band agreement. Neither a full-group opportunity nor a partial-group window becomes a rehearsal automatically.

A confirmed rehearsal records timing and explicitly identifies expected/available members. A partial-group window may be confirmed, but full-group and partial-attendance rehearsals remain distinguishable. The unresolved question for an individual candidate is whether everyone will attend, not whether partial windows are displayed.

## Rationale

Coordination information and agreed commitments have different meanings; lightweight explicit confirmation is sufficient for the MVP.

## Examples

A three-member window remains coordination information until an active member confirms it with those members explicitly identified. The resulting shared rehearsal is marked as partial attendance and visible to the band, not represented as a full-group rehearsal.

## Exceptions

No administrator role, granular confirmation permission or messaging platform is required. Non-members and inactive members cannot confirm.
