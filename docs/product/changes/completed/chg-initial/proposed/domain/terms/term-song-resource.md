## Definition

A linked or repository-held item supporting a song, such as sheet music, lyrics, chords, reference audio, rehearsal recording, video or a Google document.

## Distinguish From

A requirement to copy large media into Git or to make private material public.

## Usage

Accessible musical references alongside song details.
