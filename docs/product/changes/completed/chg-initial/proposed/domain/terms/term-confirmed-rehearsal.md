## Definition

A shared rehearsal explicitly confirmed by any active member after agreement, recording timing and expected/available members. It may originate from a full-group opportunity or partial-group window; full-group and partial attendance remain distinguishable.

## Distinguish From

Rehearsal opportunity; automatic detection does not confirm attendance.

## Usage

Upcoming rehearsal visibility and preparation of songs and focus.
