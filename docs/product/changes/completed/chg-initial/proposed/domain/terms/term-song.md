## Definition

A musical piece in Pintatónica's repertoire with band-specific metadata, notes and associated resources.

## Distinguish From

A media file; a song may link several external resources.

## Usage

Title, original artist, status, key, tempo, structure or arrangement notes, band notes and public/private visibility may be recorded.
