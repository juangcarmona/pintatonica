## Intended Outcome

Find band song information and musical references in an organised home.

## Entry Conditions

An authorised member wants to work on repertoire.

## Journey Narrative

Enter privately, find a song, read its musical and band-specific information and open relevant resources.

## Variants and Branches

A resource may live in Google Docs, Drive, YouTube or repository files. Availability depends on its owner's permissions; public selection is a separate decision.

## Completion Conditions

The needed song metadata and accessible references can be used without unnecessary media duplication.
