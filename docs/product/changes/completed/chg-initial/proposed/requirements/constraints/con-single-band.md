## Constraint

The product serves Pintatónica with a public band website and the private authenticated /band member area. It remains small and pragmatic. It is not a generic multi-tenant band SaaS, social network, messaging platform, WhatsApp or Drive replacement, full calendar, ticketing or streaming platform, project-management tool or complex editorial CMS.

## Rationale

Juan's supplied scope intentionally limits the product to this band's musical organisation and public presence.

## Consequences

Membership may change without requiring multi-band tenancy. Musical preparation stays lightweight. Link external material rather than requiring large-media duplication. The initial MVP includes Google sign-in and active membership; /band; recurring weekly availability, date overrides and multiple intervals; fixed six-week planning; full-group opportunities and mandatory separate partial windows; explicit rehearsal confirmation and upcoming rehearsals; repertoire/resources; editable rehearsal focus; setlists; and basic gig/public information. Defer Google Calendar sync, notifications, messaging/chat, automated membership administration, granular permissions, horizon/duration configurability, multi-band tenancy, advanced events, media hosting and replacement of Drive/Docs. Concrete technologies and delivery slices are not product requirements.
