## Purpose

An explicitly authorised current Pintatónica member coordinating and preparing the band's musical activity.

## Goals

Find rehearsal windows, see confirmed rehearsals, prepare songs and use repertoire resources and setlists.

## Responsibilities

Maintain their availability and contribute to rehearsal and performance preparation within agreed permissions.

## Boundaries

Google authentication alone does not establish membership. An active member may confirm rehearsals and edit shared repertoire, rehearsal focus and setlists, while editing only their own availability. Membership is manually provisioned outside normal product flows; no administrator product role is introduced. Guille, Juan, Will and Pablo are current evidence, not a fixed cardinality or immutable roster.
