## Purpose

Discover Pintatónica through its public presence.

## Goals

Learn who the band is, explore selected music and media, find upcoming gigs and contact information.

## Responsibilities

Browse public information and use the published contact route.

## Boundaries

Public browsing does not grant private membership or access to band organisation.
