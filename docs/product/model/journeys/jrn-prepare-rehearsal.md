## Intended Outcome

Arrive knowing the rehearsal timing, expected people, songs and musical focus.

## Entry Conditions

An upcoming rehearsal is confirmed.

## Journey Narrative

Open the member area, inspect and update the upcoming rehearsal’s focus, lightweight notes and songs, then open relevant song details and resources. Any active member may save shared preparation updates.

## Variants and Branches

Focus may be incomplete; any active member may complete or update it. External resources may need their own access.

## Completion Conditions

The member can use the band's shared preparation context without project-management machinery.
