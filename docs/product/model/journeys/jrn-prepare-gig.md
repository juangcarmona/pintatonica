## Intended Outcome

Prepare an ordered musical running order and useful performance context.

## Entry Conditions

The band has a gig or rehearsal to prepare.

## Journey Narrative

Review repertoire, choose and order songs, record approximate duration and performance notes, and associate the setlist with the intended gig or rehearsal.

## Variants and Branches

Any active member may edit shared setlists. Gig handling is limited to basic public information and musical preparation; advanced event management is deferred.

## Completion Conditions

The band has a lightweight usable musical plan; public gig display remains separate from private notes.
