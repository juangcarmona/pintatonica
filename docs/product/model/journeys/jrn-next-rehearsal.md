## Intended Outcome

Turn accurate availability into an actually agreed next rehearsal.

## Entry Conditions

A member has Google identity and explicit band membership.

## Journey Narrative

Enter privately, set habitual availability, add date exceptions, inspect computed overlap and discuss the opportunities with the band. Any active member explicitly confirms the agreed full-group or partial candidate, identifying expected/available members; the shared rehearsal becomes visible with its attendance distinction.

## Variants and Branches

Availability may be missing or no full-group window may qualify. Separate partial windows are mandatory. Any active member may confirm a full-group or partial candidate; whether everyone attends is settled explicitly for that rehearsal, with expected/available members shown. The MVP minimum full-group opportunity duration is two continuous hours. Agreement may happen outside the product.

## Completion Conditions

An agreed rehearsal is visible with timing and expected participants, distinct from possible windows.
