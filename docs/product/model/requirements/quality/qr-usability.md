## Requirement

The product MUST support responsive, mobile-first use and good desktop usability across its public and private journeys. Public pages MUST preserve a polished band identity; the private area MUST make musical coordination practical.

## Measurement

Demonstrate the principal journeys on representative mobile and desktop viewports, reviewing legibility, navigation, usable controls and scheduling information with Juan. Specific device coverage and accessibility targets can be refined without inventing them here.

Demonstrate that the single public page tracks its currently visible section after scrolling and link activation, including shared links and browser history. Demonstrate that private navigation accurately indicates the current page, that the Backstage entry clearly communicates member-only sign-in, and that separate private area pages support direct entry, refresh and browser history. Area navigation is usable with a keyboard and does not silently discard unsaved edits. Public and private areas sharing a name remain distinguishable in context.
