## Requirement

The product MUST support responsive, mobile-first use and good desktop usability across its public and private journeys. Public pages MUST preserve a polished band identity; the private area MUST make musical coordination practical.

## Measurement

Demonstrate the principal journeys on representative mobile and desktop viewports, reviewing legibility, navigation, usable controls and scheduling information with Juan. Specific device coverage and accessibility targets can be refined without inventing them here.
