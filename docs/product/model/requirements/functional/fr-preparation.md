## Requirement

The product MUST support shared preparation for confirmed rehearsals answering when, who is expected/available, which songs and what needs work. Any active member MUST be able to view and update songs to work on and lightweight notes/focus for that rehearsal; saved updates MUST be available to other members.

Any active member MUST be able to create and edit lightweight ordered setlists for rehearsals and gigs, with approximate duration and useful musical/performance notes. Keep preparation private even when the related gig is public. Advanced event management and task/project-management machinery are excluded.

## Rationale

Make musical preparation useful collaboratively while keeping it lightweight.
