## Definition

A dated or recurring interval with a start and end time used to express availability or rehearsal timing.

## Distinguish From

An opportunity or an agreed rehearsal; a slot alone asserts neither.

## Usage

Multiple intervals may exist on one day; schedules use Madrid time.
