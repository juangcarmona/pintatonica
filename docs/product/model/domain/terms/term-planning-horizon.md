## Definition

The fixed rolling six-week collection of weeks and dates used to inspect availability, full-group opportunities and separate partial windows in the MVP.

## Distinguish From

A user-configurable horizon; configurability is explicitly deferred.

## Usage

Schedule inspection and qualifying full-group week highlighting.
