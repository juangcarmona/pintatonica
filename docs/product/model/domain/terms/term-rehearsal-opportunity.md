## Definition

A primary full-group possibility calculated when all active Pintatónica members overlap continuously for at least two hours. All active members are required; no fixed four-person count or user-configurable duration policy applies in the MVP.

## Distinguish From

A confirmed rehearsal requires explicit confirmation. A partial-group window contains at least two but fewer than all active members and is displayed separately as coordination information; it may later be explicitly confirmed as a partial-attendance rehearsal.

## Usage

Primary scheduling candidates and full-group week highlighting. Separate partial windows show participants and duration and never acquire full-group semantics.
