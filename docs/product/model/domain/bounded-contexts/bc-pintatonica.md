## Responsibility

The language of this band's public presence, membership, rehearsal coordination, repertoire and lightweight performance preparation.

## Language

One band, its current members, their shared musical material and public-facing content. This language boundary does not prescribe implementation modules or storage.

## Boundaries

No generic multi-tenant service, messaging, streaming, ticketing or project management.

## External Relationships

Google supplies sign-in identity; membership is a separate band decision. Google Docs, Drive and YouTube may hold linked material. Google Calendar synchronization is explicitly deferred beyond the MVP.
