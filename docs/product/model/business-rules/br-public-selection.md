## Rule

Songs may explicitly be marked public/private. Only songs editorially selected for public display are exposed. Public song data may include title, original artist and suitable public-facing media/content.

Private notes, rehearsal material, sheet music, working links and internal resources MUST NOT become public automatically because a song is public. A public gig listing likewise does not expose internal preparation.

## Rationale

The band's public presence and private organisation serve different audiences.

## Examples

Selecting a song for public display exposes its intended public title, artist and suitable media, while internal arrangement notes, rehearsal recordings and working resource links remain protected. A private song is not exposed in public repertoire.

## Exceptions

The exact public song list is an editorial choice, not fixed in the product model. No all-repertoire default or complex editorial CMS is required.
