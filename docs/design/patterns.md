# Patterns

Provisional layout patterns derived from the logo.

- **Bar rhythm.** A row of equal-width solid blocks with slightly varied heights on a shared baseline, used for hero areas, section dividers and loading states.
- **Polarity pairs.** Alternate paper and ink sections; the wordmark appears in the matching inverse colour.
- **Colour assignment.** Blue, green, yellow and red are an unordered set; when assigning them to categories, pair each with a label and keep the assignment stable across the site.
- **Spacing.** Gutters and padding come from `--space-*`; stagger heights, not gutters.
- **Media.** Imagery sits in square-cornered frames without overlays or shadows; text over images needs a solid ink or paper block behind it.
- **Motion.** Instant or short transitions; honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.
