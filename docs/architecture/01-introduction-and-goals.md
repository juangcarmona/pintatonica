# Introduction and Goals

## Requirements Overview

[The accepted ProductShape baseline](../product/model/) owns product intent; [CHG-INITIAL](../product/changes/completed/chg-initial/change.md) records its application. GH-1 introduces an executable public and member-area shell, not the product journeys themselves. Private access and musical workflows are subsequent delivery slices.

## Quality Goals

[Section 10](10-quality-requirements.md) maps governing quality artifacts to mechanisms and evidence. The shell establishes a static delivery path, token reuse and browser-only Firebase startup without claiming the complete MVP is implemented.

## Stakeholders

Juan owns product and delivery acceptance. Humans and agents use the [engineering lifecycle](../engineering-lifecycle.md), accepted ProductShape artifacts and architecture evidence together.
