# Introduction and Goals

## Requirements Overview

This repository is in product definition and infrastructure/bootstrap work. [CHG-INITIAL](../product/changes/active/chg-initial/change.md) is the proposed product-intent workspace; [the accepted model](../product/model/) contains no artifacts. No public site or private application UI exists. Firebase client initialization and data-security scaffolding now exist alongside repository support, as described in [05](05-building-block-view.md).

Proposed actors, behaviours and domain terms belong to ProductShape, not this architecture. Links to proposed artifacts are investigation pointers, not accepted product citations. Observed implementation does not itself accept product intent. [Infrastructure notes](infrastructure.md) report platform acceptance and provisioning; formal decision records and cloud-state verification are not established by this documentation pass.

## Quality Goals

Architecture evaluation awaits product acceptance. The candidate drivers and current realization gaps are mapped in [10](10-quality-requirements.md); they have not been prioritized as accepted architectural goals. The current repository establishes traceable ownership and reproducible local verification, not proof of application security, cost or usability.

## Stakeholders

Juan reviews product and architectural decisions. Human and agent contributors need evidence-backed boundaries, explicit uncertainties and navigable decisions. Their delivery responsibilities are owned by the [engineering lifecycle](../engineering-lifecycle.md); product actors are owned by ProductShape.
